How it works

Three steps. Permanent result.

01 / ISSUE
🏛️

Institution issues

An authorised institution connects their wallet and calls issueCredential — minting a soulbound NFT directly to the recipient's wallet.

02 / BIND
🔒

Token is bound

The token locks permanently to the recipient's wallet. No transfer will ever succeed — enforced at the contract level by EIP-5192.

03 / VERIFY
✅

Anyone verifies

Employers, institutions, or individuals query the blockchain with a wallet address and retrieve verified credentials instantly.
